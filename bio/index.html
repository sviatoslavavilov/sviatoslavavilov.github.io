Biography

Sviatoslav Avilov is a Venice-based composer, sound and multimedia artist. His work moves across acoustic composition, live electronics, spatial sound sculpture, and generative audiovisual performance.

He is the founder of the musical projects The Whale Project and Goid, curating immersive audiovisual environments presented at national and international festivals, art biennials, and concert venues across Europe.

Recent works include the audiovisual cycle Ebb and Flow, for live ensemble and real-time generative visuals developed in part during his research in crystallography and material modeling, and the contemporary opera Void in collaboration with librettist Leonardo Piana.

His music for interactive installations and performances — including Ethereal, The Unbearable Lightness,We ARE, Satellite, and Fossile — has been presented at the Florence Biennale, the Venice Design Biennale, Venice Art Night, CaminoControCorrente, and FestArt Venezia.
