Ebb and Flow

Four-part audiovisual cycle exploring process self-similarity across universal scales: 1. Crystal (nano-micro crystals), 2. Arboretum (growth of trees and human existence), 3. Mountain (petrification and purification), 4. Ab Infinito (cosmos and return to the origin).
