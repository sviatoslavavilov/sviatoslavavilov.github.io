Ebb and Flow

Audiovisual cycle exploring process self-similarity across universal scales. The music traverses deep cosmic space, rigid crystal formations, organic growth, geological time scales, and infinite recursive loops.

Beast

Single for electric guitar, clarinet, and live electronics. Inspired by the expressive clarinet playing of Cristina Scapol, exploring the theme of crocodile tears, deceit, and hypocrisy.
