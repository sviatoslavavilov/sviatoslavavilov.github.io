Ebb and Flow

CaminoControCorrente Festival · Venice Art Night
live ensemble, live electronics and real-time generative visuals
composition · conducting · coding

Four-part audiovisual cycle exploring process self-similarity across universal scales, featuring geometry notation for fractal visuals generated with original Multiplato software.

I. Crystal
Explores micro-structures and rigid formations.
II. Arboretum
Organic growth and branching patterns.
III. Mountain
Geological time scales and massive tectonic shifts.
IV. Ab Infinito
Cosmic scales and infinite recursion.
