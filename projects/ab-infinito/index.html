Ab Infinito

2023–2024  ·  organ, piano, live visuals and live electronics
composition · organ · live visuals

Fourth movement of the Ebb and Flow cycle. Explores cosmic transfiguration and recursive returns through mechanical pipe organ textures and real-time DSP.

Performance & Production Notes

  • — Performed at Venice Art Night 2023, the 66th and 71st editions of the 60 dB Festival of Contemporary Music, and CaminoControCorrente 2024 in Villa di Varmo.
