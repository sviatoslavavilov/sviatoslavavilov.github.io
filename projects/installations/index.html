Sound Sculpture & Installations

2023 — Present
site-specific sound · interactive electronics · sculpture

Site-specific sound installations combining spatial audio systems, vibrating sculpture surfaces (Dayton exciters, bass shakers), interactive proximity sensing, and original music compositions. Developed for art biennials and festivals in Venice, Florence, and Friuli.

The Unbearable Lightness

Oct 2023 · Florence Biennale of Art and Design 2023
multi-channel sound system · TouchDesigner · plugdata OSC
music composition · sound production · technical setup · programming

Installation with a collage of music pieces by Sviatoslav Avilov and Anna Dobrucka. Sound tracks include Morning Tide (vibraphone), Acqua Alta (harp), and Underwater (electronics). Technical setup of DMX lighting controllers and plugdata VST plugins sending OSC from Reaper to TouchDesigner.
