Void

Contemporary Opera · 2024 — Present
composition · sound sculpture · scenography · conducting

Contemporary opera in collaboration with librettist Leonardo Piana. Sound sculptures and resonating instruments developed in collaboration with students of the Accademia di Belle Arti di Venezia.

Second act premiered at Venice Art Night 2025 with visuals of the IndraSync art collective. Full scenes premiered at CaminoControCorrente 2026.
