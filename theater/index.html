Contemporary opera in collaboration with librettist Leonardo Piana. Investigating alternative amplified acoustic instruments through custom sound sculptures developed with Lorenzo De Metrio and Giorgio Severin. The second act was premiered at Venice Art Night with visuals by the IndraSync art collective. Selected scenes premiered at the CaminoControCorrente Contemporary Music Festival in 2026 in Camino al Tagliamento.
